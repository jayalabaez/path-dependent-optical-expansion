# Archived PDOE hypothesis

The repository began as an investigation of whether spherical wavefront growth or a photon-only optical background could replace metric cosmic expansion.

That program produced useful falsification results, but its central physical claims were rejected:

- wavefront area growth changes amplitude, not radial wavelength;
- circumference coupling is origin- and beam-geometry-dependent;
- smooth photon-only optical redshift fails multimessenger timing;
- static Euclidean implementations fail distance-duality and geometry tests;
- the only broad loophole becomes a universal evolving effective metric.

The historical simulations and audits remain in the repository for transparency. They are no longer the primary research claim.
